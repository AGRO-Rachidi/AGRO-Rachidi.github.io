About me

I'm a final-year student in the engineering programme in Mathematical Engineering and Modeling at ENSGMM (UNSTIM), in Abomey, Benin. Before that I spent two years in the national engineering preparatory classes (INSPEI).

Mathematics trained me to model a problem before solving it. Systems and networks are where I like to apply that habit: they are concrete, they fail in precise ways, and every failure has a cause you can find. That is what pulled me toward cybersecurity, and toward the defensive side in particular, where the job is to understand an environment well enough to notice when something is wrong.

Most of what I know in security I learned on my own: Linux on my own machine, Kali in a virtual lab, Cisco Networking Academy, TryHackMe, and a lot of reading. In 2026 I completed a two-month internship in network and systems administration at FusionSoft Labs in Parakou, which gave that self-study a structure: real specifications, deadlines, and supervisors who asked me to justify each choice.

My method is simple. I start from what the system actually shows, not from what I expect. I make one change at a time, I test it in both directions, and I write down what happened. When something breaks, I treat it as information, not as a detour.

I want to work in a security team, first in operations (SOC, Blue Team), and grow toward security engineering. I'm also curious about how AI changes both attacks and defence, and I study it with that question in mind.
