About me

I'm Tchêgoun Rachidi AGRO, a final-year student in Mathematical Engineering and Modeling at ENSGMM (UNSTIM) in Abomey, Benin. I expect to graduate in 2027.

My engineering track is mostly mathematics: statistics, modeling, optimization. Along the way I became more and more curious about how computer systems actually work, and how they fail. That's how Linux, networking and security ended up taking most of my free time.

In the summer of 2026 I did a two-month internship at FusionSoft Labs in Parakou. Two instructors gave us lessons on networking and Linux administration, and the rest of the time I practiced on my own. That's when I worked through a series of network labs and ran a few projects of my own: hardening a Linux server, building a file server with per-department access, designing a multi-site network. All of them were done in simulated environments (Docker, Cisco Packet Tracer), and that's how I present them here.

The area I want to grow in is defensive security: securing Linux systems, understanding networks, and eventually working in a SOC or Blue Team. I've also started looking at how AI is being used in cybersecurity; I'm still at the very beginning there.
